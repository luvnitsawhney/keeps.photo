Questions.

Can I keep it a surprise?
Yes. Only people with the link can add to it, and we never contact the birthday person. Just don't post the link in a chat they're in.
The party is tomorrow. Is that too late?
No. Set uploads to close tonight or early tomorrow. Your film is ready within an hour of them closing, day or night.
When does the hour start?
When uploads close: at the deadline you set, or earlier if you close them yourself once everyone's in. Your film is ready within the hour after that, day or night.
Do people need an app or an account?
No. It's a web link that opens on any phone or computer. They add their name, then record a video or upload photos.
What if Grandma can't use the link?
She can send her video to you on WhatsApp instead. Save it and upload it on your page with her name, so it's credited to her.
Can I choose what goes in?
Yes. Tell us their story, the moments that must be in it, the opening title and the closing line. We build the film around that.
How long is the film?
Usually 5–10 minutes, depending on how much everyone sends. Long enough to tell the story, short enough to watch together.
Can people far away watch it?
Yes. Anyone who leaves their email when they send something can get a link to watch the film. You choose when it goes out, so it stays a surprise. The film stays up for 30 days, so download a copy to keep.
What music do you use?
Tracks that suit the mood you pick, from celebratory to heartfelt. Say more in your brief and we'll match it.
What if I don't love it?
Email us what to change and we'll remake it once, free. If it's still not right, you get a full refund.
Are the photos and videos private?
Yes. Uploads go to private storage that only Keeps can open, and they're deleted 30 days after your film is delivered. See the privacy policy.
