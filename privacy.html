This explains what Keeps, operated by Keeps.photo, Bangalore, India, collects and why.

What we collect

  • From you: your name, email, optional phone number and the brief you write.
  • From the people you invite: the name they type, the photos and videos they upload, and their email only if they choose to give it.
  • From our payment processor (Razorpay, or PayPal if you choose it): your email, phone number, name or country where they share it, and payment confirmation. We never see your card details.

Why

Only to make and deliver your film, contact you about the order, and handle refunds and remakes. We send a few emails about your order: your private link, when your film is ready, a reminder before files are deleted, and one question about how it went. No marketing to you. People who leave their email on the upload page get the finished film when it's shared, and nothing else. Ideas and offers from Keeps only go to people who choose them with a separate box: it's unticked to start, except in the US, where it starts ticked and you can untick it; we keep a record of that choice, and they can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email. We also note whether your film was downloaded, so we only remind you when it matters.

Where it's stored

Uploads and order details are stored privately with Supabase (cloud hosting). Invite links let people add files but not view anyone else's. Payments are processed by Razorpay, or by PayPal if you choose it. Emails are sent through Resend.

How long

All uploaded photos, videos and the finished film are deleted 30 days after delivery. Order and payment records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time, including deleting files early. If you're in the UK or EU you also have rights under the GDPR, including complaining to your data protection authority.

Analytics and cookies

We use PostHog to count visits and see which steps people complete, so we can improve the site. It stores nothing on your device, never receives your private order or invite links, and doesn't record anything you type. After you order, your visits are linked to your order number (never your name or email), so we can see which steps work and which don't. It also records page errors so we can fix them. We advertise on Google, so our public pages (not your order or upload pages) use Google Tag Manager and Google Ads cookies to see which ads lead to a purchase. When you buy, your email is turned into a one-way code (hashed) in your browser and sent to Google so it can match the sale to an ad; Google can't read the email from it. In the UK, EU and Switzerland these stay off unless you tap Accept, and you can decline. Your private order and upload links are never shared with Google. Razorpay and PayPal set their own cookies during checkout.
