08 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long should my clips be?
Anything from a few seconds to a few minutes. We'll pick the best moments.
What if I only have photos?
That works. We'll build a sequence with gentle movement and music.
When does the hour start?
When uploads close. After you pay, you choose when that happens, or close them yourself once everyone has sent theirs. Your film arrives within an hour, day or night.
How long is the film?
Usually 5–10 minutes, depending on how much you send. Long enough to tell the story, short enough to watch together at the party.
Can family send their own clips?
Yes. You get a private link to share on WhatsApp or by email. Anyone with it can add photos and video messages with their name until your deadline, without an app or an account.
What if someone sends their video to me instead?
That's fine, and common with older relatives. Save it from WhatsApp and upload it on your page with their name, so it's credited to them in the film.
Can I request changes?
We aim to get it right first time from your notes. If something isn't right, email us and we'll remake it once, free.
What music do you use?
Tracks that suit the occasion: warm, reflective or celebratory. Tell us the feeling you want in your brief.
Is a real person making it?
Yes. Every film is edited by hand, which is how it can be fast and still feel personal.
Are my photos private?
Yes. Uploads go to private storage that only our editor can open, and they're deleted 30 days after your film is delivered. We never share your film without asking. See the privacy policy.
