One free remake

If the film isn't right, tap Not quite right? on your film page (or email us) and tell us what to change. We'll remake it once at no cost.

Full refund

If the remake still isn't right, we'll refund you in full. Just reply and say so.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with. We start the refund the same day you ask.

  • Razorpay: usually 5–7 working days to reach your account. International cards can take longer, up to about 20 working days, depending on your bank.
  • PayPal: usually within a few days. If PayPal funded it from a card, your card issuer may take up to 30 days to show it.

If it hasn't arrived after that, email us and we'll send the refund reference so your bank can trace it.
